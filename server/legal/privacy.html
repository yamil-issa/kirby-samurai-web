Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

This policy explains what data is used by the "Samurai Kirby" Discord Activity and how.

1. Data used

When you launch the Activity, Discord asks you to authorize access to your Discord user ID and display name (OAuth2 scope identify). This display name is used only to show it above your character during the match.

2. What is NOT collected or stored

  • No database: match information (name, result) exists only in memory for the duration of the match, then is discarded.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising tools.
  • No data is shared with any third party.

3. Discord access token

The access token provided by Discord upon login is used server-side only to verify your identity, and is neither stored nor retained after your session ends.

4. Your rights

Since no personal data is stored durably, there is nothing to delete or export on our end. For any questions, contact noyzzmaker@gmail.com.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top of the page reflects the latest revision.
